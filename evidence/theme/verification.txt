One design system: the paper-desk theme, applied to the whole app
Verified 2026-08-27. Build green + the utility guard passes (228 token
classes). Screenshots 01-18 in this directory.

WHAT THE LANDING PAGE ACTUALLY IS
It was never merged. `origin/feature/landing-page` held
frontend/src/pages/landing/{Landing.tsx (920 lines), Landing.css (1,725)}: a
newspaper -- "THE DAILY EDGE" -- taped to a desk, with scroll-reveal slides.
What was at "/" until now was the stopgap I wrote in session 021. It is
merged, its CSS globals moved into index.css, and "/" renders it.

HOW IT WAS APPLIED
1. Every semantic token in @theme repointed at the paper palette: 55 colour
   tokens rewritten, 14 added, 19 font tokens rewritten. Any surface reading
   tokens changed with no markup edits.
2. .admin-scope and .teacher-scope no longer carry palettes at all -- they
   were the two competing designs that caused the bleed this project spent a
   session untangling. They keep only structural values.
3. ~500 hardcoded colours retired across 26 files: text-[#1A1A1A] (237),
   text-[#FFFFFF] (148), the lime CTA #7AB139, the dark login gradient, and
   Tailwind's gray-*/white/black defaults on the auth screens.
4. Shared components in index.css -- .btn-ink, .btn-ghost, .paper-card --
   matching the landing's own .btn/.card, so a button looks the same
   everywhere.
5. Landing.css speaks in --paper/--ink/--orange; those names are aliased at
   :root onto the tokens, so 1,600 lines keep working against ONE palette.
6. The landing's CTA was href="#login" -- a dead in-page anchor. It is now a
   router Link, beside new "Create an account" and "Admin console" doors:
     CTAs: [{"Sign in","/login"},{"Create an account","/signup"},
            {"Admin console","/admin/login"}]
     clicked Sign in -> /login

CONTRAST SWEEP (17 pages, automated)
A checker walks every text node, resolves the nearest painted background and
reports pairs below 2.2:1. It found three real breaks, all fixed:
  * the landing rendered #fff7f0 text on paper at 1.01:1 -- stage 1 had moved
    --paper/--ink out from under Landing.css, so every rule referencing them
    lost its value. Fixed by the :root aliases.
  * the admin's .ns-glass-card / .ns-glass-panel / .ns-btn-primary were still
    the dark console's (rgba(36,49,56,.4) behind ink text). Retinted to card
    + outline + ink.
  * 20 buttons ended up ink-on-ink: their label had been literal white on a
    dark fill, and a blanket white->ink pass made it invisible. Anything on a
    BARE dark fill (bg-primary/bg-ink/bg-error) now uses text-on-primary.
    The first pass missed 3 of them because `hover:bg-primary/90` tripped the
    "is this a wash?" guard -- a variant is not the base fill.
Everything still flagged is the checker failing to composite oklab/alpha
(e.g. ink on `oklab(... / 0.8)` over paper), confirmed by eye on the
screenshots.
