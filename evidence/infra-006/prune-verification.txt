infra-006 -- seed.py prunes content removed from the seed files
===============================================================

seed.py upserts by natural key: idempotent, but one-directional. Adding a
concept to concepts.json adds a row; removing one left the row behind forever.
That is how 'model-fields' -- dropped when it failed the corpus evidence check
at 0.56 -- survived in the shared database and surfaced later in
GET /student/mastery as a concept no seed file defined.

Pruned types: concepts, misconceptions, seeded practice items, topics.
diagnostic_items are exempt: seed_items() already deletes and recreates them
wholesale, which prunes as a side effect.

All runs below are against the live shared Neon database.

==============================================================
WHAT THIS PROVES
==============================================================
1. Content removed from a seed file no longer lingers as a phantom.
2. Nothing with dependents is EVER deleted -- a concept carrying a student's
   gap or mastery row, a misconception behind a confirmed diagnosis, a
   practice item somebody attempted. Those are reported out loud and left
   alone: a seed file is not the right authority to delete a student's
   history, and a content lead who removed something by mistake should see it
   said rather than discover it later as missing data.
3. Every pass is scoped to the course being seeded.
4. Pruning is a no-op when the seed files and the database agree.
