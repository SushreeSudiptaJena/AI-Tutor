READING
-------
All 8 PREREQUISITE concepts are sufficient. Every diagnostic item can therefore
produce a teachable gap lesson -- golden path step 2 cannot refuse.

python-classes-objects (align 0.4591) and html-forms (align 0.4892) FAIL, which
is why they carry no prerequisite_course and are not tested by the diagnostic.
They remain listed as concepts because the seeded practice items and the
misconceptions behind 7 confirmed heatmap diagnoses hang off them; seed.py's
add_diagnoses() silently skips a misconception whose problem_type has no
practice item, so deleting them would have emptied two heatmap rows with no
error anywhere.

THIN MARGIN -- url-routing, top-sim 0.7274 against a 0.70 gate (+0.027).
It is the weakest prerequisite and it is now both a diagnostic item and a
gap_counts entry. calibrate_threshold.py suggests 0.72 for this corpus; at 0.72
url-routing clears by 0.0074. This is a concrete reason NOT to take that
suggestion. Re-measure this concept specifically after any re-ingest.
