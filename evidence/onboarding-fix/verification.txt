Onboarding flow correction — 2026-08-27
Owner report: "/onboarding/1 shouldn't have an admin option, and the page must
not be displayed for a teacher — that's part of signup, and teachers log in
directly. Same for a student who is signing in: only after signup."

VERIFIED (headless Edge, live):
  teacher forced to /onboarding/1 ends at: /teacher
  existing student signing in lands at:    /dashboard
  SIGNUP step 1: {"path":"/onboarding/1","hasAdminOption":false,
                  "roleCards":["Student"],"teacherNote":true}
  step 2 continues into: /onboarding/course   (was /login before)

WHAT WAS WRONG
1. The Admin card. Admin accounts are seeded and there is no admin signup, so
   the option could never do anything -- and the answer was discarded anyway
   ("You can later save selectedRole").
2. The page was reachable by anyone. /onboarding/1 and /2 had NO guard.
3. **The admin login door sent every non-admin to /onboarding/1.** A teacher
   or a returning student who used /admin/login was shown "Who are you?"
   instead of their console. This is the bug behind the report.
4. Two further bugs found while fixing it, neither reported:
   * both welcome steps dead-ended at navigate("/login") -- a student who
     signed up and walked them was returned to the login screen they had just
     come through, which reads as "your signup failed";
   * RequireAuth sent a wrong-role user to /login, so a signed-in teacher who
     typed the URL was asked to log in again.

WHAT CHANGED
* Onboarding1: Admin card deleted. The Teacher card is now a NOTE, not a
  choice: "Your department admin creates your account and shares your
  password — just log in, no signup needed." A card that cannot be acted on
  is worse than a sentence that explains why.
* /onboarding/1 and /2 are RequireAuth role="student", and RequireAuth now
  redirects a wrong-role user to their OWN surface (/admin, /teacher,
  /dashboard) rather than to a login screen.
* Signup -> /onboarding/1 -> /onboarding/2 -> /onboarding/course ->
  diagnostic. The welcome steps are reachable only from signup.
* Both admin and unified login doors route by role: admin -> /admin,
  teacher -> /teacher, student -> /dashboard (or /onboarding/course when not
  yet enrolled). Neither ever routes into the welcome steps.
